概觀：@OpenAPIDefinition #

  • info = info/Info.class
    • contact = info/Contact.class
    • license = info/License.class
  • ExternalDocumentation.class
  • servers/Server.class
    • servers/Servers.class
    • servers/ServerVariable.class
  • tags/Tag.class
    • tags/Tags.class
  • security/SecurityRequirement.class
    • security/SecurityRequirements.class
